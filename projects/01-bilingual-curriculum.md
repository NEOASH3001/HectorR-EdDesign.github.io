# Bilingual/ESL Curriculum Development
Curriculum Design · Elementary Education

Led curriculum development for Science and Bilingual/ESL programs that improved instructional consistency and contributed to a 16% increase in 5th-grade math STAAR scores.

## The Challenge
Teachers needed clearer, more practical curriculum pathways to support bilingual and ESL students while meeting state standards and improving academic outcomes.

## The Solution
- Developed structured curriculum maps and instructional materials
- Created practical tools for classroom implementation
- Focused on clarity, cultural responsiveness, and measurable student progress
- Collaborated with teachers, administrators, and district leadership

## What I Built
- Curriculum scope and sequence documents
- Instructional materials and teacher support resources
- Data-informed action plans
- Professional development content for staff

## Impact
Contributed to a 16% increase in 5th-grade math STAAR scores. Strengthened consistency and quality of bilingual/ESL instruction across the department.

## My Role
Curriculum Development · Instructional Design · Content Development · Stakeholder Collaboration

Led the design and development process while partnering with teachers, administrators, and district teams to ensure materials were practical and aligned to both student needs and state requirements.
