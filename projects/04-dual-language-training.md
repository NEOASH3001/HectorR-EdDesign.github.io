# Dual-Language / Onboarding-Style Training
Instructional Design · Teacher Support · Onboarding

Practical training and job aids designed to support dual-language teachers and new staff in implementing consistent instructional practices.

## The Challenge
New and existing dual-language teachers needed clear, practical guidance to implement consistent instructional practices across classrooms. Without structured support and easy-to-use tools, variation in approach risked uneven student experiences and slower onboarding for new staff.

## The Solution
- Developed practical training sessions focused on dual-language instructional practices
- Created job aids and reference tools that teachers could use immediately in the classroom
- Emphasized clarity, consistency, and ease of implementation
- Supported both new staff onboarding and ongoing development for current dual-language teachers

## What I Built
- Training content and session materials
- Job aids and quick-reference tools for classroom use
- Structured guidance on consistent dual-language instructional practices
- Supporting resources that helped standardize expectations across teachers

## Impact
Improved consistency in dual-language instruction and accelerated readiness for new staff. Provided teachers with practical tools that reduced ambiguity and supported more reliable implementation of key instructional practices.

## My Role
Instructional Design · Content Development · Teacher Support · Onboarding Design

Designed and developed the training materials and job aids while serving in dual-language liaison and curriculum support roles. Partnered with teachers and campus leaders to ensure the resources were practical, clear, and aligned with classroom realities.
