# Instructional Materials Quality Evaluation
Instructional Design · Educational Publishing · Curriculum Alignment

Served as an Instructional Materials Quality Evaluator for the Texas Education Agency, reviewing and assessing instructional content for quality, accuracy, cultural responsiveness, and alignment to state standards.

## The Challenge
School districts and educators needed reliable instructional materials that met rigorous quality standards, supported diverse learners, and aligned correctly with the Texas curriculum framework. Inconsistent or low-quality materials risked gaps in instruction and student outcomes.

## The Solution
- Conducted systematic evaluations of instructional materials submitted for state review
- Applied clear criteria focused on instructional quality, accuracy, accessibility, and standards alignment
- Provided structured feedback to support continuous improvement of educational resources
- Worked within a formal state-level review process serving districts across Texas

## What I Built / Contributed
- Detailed evaluation reports and quality assessments
- Criteria-based feedback on content strength, clarity, and alignment
- Recommendations that informed adoption and improvement decisions
- Consistent application of evaluation standards across multiple materials and subject areas

## Impact
Supported the state’s effort to ensure higher-quality instructional materials reached Texas classrooms. Contributed to more consistent, standards-aligned resources for teachers and students over a multi-year review cycle (2018–2021).

Instructional Materials Evaluation · Quality Assurance · Curriculum Alignment · Content Analysis
