# Camp Lucha – Animation Project
Storytelling · Content Development · Animation Development

Concept and development work for an original animated series developed with Powerhouse Animation Studios, focused on character-driven storytelling and visual world-building.

## The Challenge
An original animated concept needed clear character foundations, world rules, and story direction that could support a series format while remaining distinctive and appealing to its target audience.

## The Solution
- Developed core concept and series direction
- Focused on strong character design and narrative hooks
- Built a foundation for episodic storytelling within a cohesive world
- Collaborated with an established animation studio on creative development

## What I Built
- Series concept and narrative framework
- Character and world development materials
- Story direction and tonal guidelines
- Supporting content for studio collaboration

## Impact
Advanced an original animated property into formal development discussions with Powerhouse Animation Studios. Strengthened skills in adapting stories for animation and collaborating within a professional studio pipeline.

## My Role
Story Development · Content Development · Creative Development · Collaboration

Led concept and story development while partnering with Powerhouse Animation Studios to shape the project for the animation medium.
