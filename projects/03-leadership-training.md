# Leadership & Cultural Responsiveness Training
Professional Development · Leadership Development · Inclusive Practice

Professional development series for administrators focused on bias awareness, cultural responsiveness, and building more inclusive school teams.

## The Challenge
School leaders needed practical tools and shared language to recognize bias, respond to cultural differences effectively, and create more inclusive environments for both staff and students. Without consistent training, efforts to improve diversity and belonging remained uneven across the organization.

## The Solution
- Designed and delivered professional development sessions for administrators
- Focused on bias awareness, cultural responsiveness, and inclusive team-building practices
- Combined discussion, reflection, and practical application so leaders could immediately use the concepts in their daily work
- Aligned the training with broader district goals around staff diversity and community representation

## What I Built
- Facilitator and participant materials for the training series
- Structured discussion guides and reflection activities
- Practical frameworks administrators could apply when leading teams and making decisions
- Supporting content that reinforced consistent expectations across leadership

## Impact
Strengthened administrators’ ability to lead with greater cultural awareness and intentionality. Supported district-wide efforts to recruit and retain a more diverse staff and to better reflect the community’s population in school leadership and culture.
